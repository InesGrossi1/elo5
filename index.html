FAQs

What is GreenLake?

GreenLake is the cloud delivering a unified platform experience that allows enterprises to simplify IT, reduce costs, transform faster, and to run and manage your entire hybrid landscape. It streamlines IT operations across multi-vendor environments, giving teams unified management of compute, storage, and networking. It unifies and secures data everywhere, maintaining control and compliance without slowing innovation. And it accelerates AI value, moving projects from pilot to production faster.
What services can I get on the GreenLake platform?

GreenLake begins with a platform that provides essential services like identity and access management and APIs, plus value-added services for optimization of FinOps, IT sustainability, and more.

On the platform, subscribe to cloud services for use cases like private cloud, AI/ML/analytics, and CloudOps and for managing infrastructure. And you can run workloads as a service at your locations with GreenLake Flex Solution, paying for only the infrastructure you use.
Which workload is GreenLake most suited for?

GreenLake is the ideal solution for workloads that are not easily migrated to the public cloud and for organizations pursuing a hybrid-by-design strategy. It delivers centralized governance, enhanced visibility, robust security, and the confidence to manage your hybrid environment with precision.

Use Cases for GreenLake:

  • AI inferencing & model tuning.
  • Edge computing.
  • Production workloads.
  • Achieve predictable costs and a low total cost of ownership (TCO).
  • Modernization of mission-critical apps.
  • Data governance & sovereignty.

Meet strict compliance requirements with localized control and visibility.

GreenLake empowers you with the control, security, and efficiency needed to run today's most demanding workloads—wherever they reside.
How do you compare GreenLake with other offers in the market?

Unlike its competition, GreenLake is platform-based and purposely designed with a common control plane to support a hybrid cloud operating model. It provides a cloud experience on premises in data centers, colocations, and edge locations. GreenLake is a hybrid-by-design cloud with services to observe, manage, automate, and secure a hybrid cloud estate. It gives customers multiple options for payment and management.
What roles do Agentic AI and AIOps play with GreenLake?

Traditional AIOps has powered GreenLake for years, providing recommendations and automated workflow based on predictive analytics and similar technology. In 2025 we introduced GreenLake Intelligence, an agentic AI framework that will act as a smart, always-on operations team. Using natural language, users can access a mesh of intelligent agents, each trained on a specific domain, that work together to troubleshoot issues, optimize infrastructure, and eventually act autonomously when given approval.
Is GreenLake only a consumption model for IT?

No. Pay-per-use1 economics are a key part of the overall GreenLake value proposition, but GreenLake enables you to manage your hybrid landscape through a unified platform used by multiple roles within your organization. You have choice in how you consume the infrastructure that you control with GreenLake: pay per use, subscription, or traditional purchase.
Can I demo GreenLake?

Yes. To get a quick feel for GreenLake, view the interactive demo. Several services offer free evaluations that you can access from the Services Catalog when logged in to GreenLake at common.cloud.hpe.com. To quickly find these, filter the catalog to Evaluation Available services.
